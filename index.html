Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android games Tractor Master (com.lutfi.tractormaster) and Archer Master (com.lutfi.archermaster), published on Google Play by Maybars Mühendislik Çözümleri ("we").

Information we collect

The games do not collect, store or share any personal or sensitive user data. They do not ask for an account, do not request any device permissions and do not connect to the internet. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party services inside the games.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stars, unlocked items, settings and best times) is saved only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Children

The games are suitable for a general audience and do not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.

Google Play

When you download the games, Google Play may process information under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive personal data from Google Play beyond anonymous, aggregated statistics provided by Google Play Console.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For any questions, please use the developer contact email shown on the games' Google Play pages.
